# From the first cell to the flight home

[Home](../../README.en.md) · [Български](PLAY_BG.md) · [All controls](../../README_EN.md)

To discover the recipes yourself, keep the hints folded and follow the goals in **Field**. To follow this particular route, start with the seed `PELAGOS`. Selecting a site sets a course. Wait for **ON COURSE** to change to **LOCAL** before choosing the next site. The resource bar shows what you collected.

## 1. Genesis — feed your cell and produce an offspring

1. In **Field**, select **Nutrient bloom**. Repeat until you have fed three times. Check the counter under **Next epoch**.
2. Open **Anatomy**, note your current speed and press **＋** beside **Flagellum**. Return to the field to try swimming with it.
3. In **Anatomy**, choose **Divide** with 4 biomass in reserve. Compare the offspring with the parent. **Inherit this variant** is optional and replaces your traits with its own.
4. When all three goals are checked, select **Evolution →** in **Field**. Use the same panel to advance in later epochs.

WASD or the arrow keys let you swim manually. Press **E** near a site to gather. Feeding restores vitality. If a predator closes in, **Space** releases a defensive pulse for 2 charge.

## 2. Evolution — establish a population on land

1. Grow an **Antenna** and an **Air sac** in **Anatomy**. Together their first levels cost 5 biomass, 1 crystal and 2 ore. Visit a **Mineral reef** for ore and a **Prism vein** for crystal.
2. Gather another 4 biomass for your population before setting **Field → Dry shore** as your destination. Wait for the landfall goal to register.
3. While still on dry ground, select **Anatomy → Found population**. Open **Archive** to see the genetic lineage, then advance to **Awakening**.

## 3. Awakening — a settlement, a tool and an ally

1. Choose a site and select **Build → Settlement** with 4 biomass and 3 ore. To find the same view later, note the planet name and the two coordinates on the strip below the field.
2. In **Lab**, select two samples, a stimulus and an intensity, then choose **Run experiment**. Compare the reading with the research notes. Every trial uses one unit of each selected sample and charge equal to the intensity.

<details>
<summary>Hint for the first tool</summary>

Select **Biomass + Ore**, **Heat**, **1**. Discovering **Tempered fibres** unlocks the **Workshop**. **Archive → Encyclopedia of discoveries** records the conditions and the planet where you made the discovery.

</details>

3. In **Society**, pick one house. Choose **Greet**, wait to arrive and check that trust has reached 1. Choose **Trade**, then **Ally** when trust reaches 2. The sequence costs 3 biomass and 2 ore in total. Each house tracks trust separately.
4. For regular supplies, build a **Garden** for biomass and brine and a **Workshop** for ore and crystal. With the settlement, tool and alliance in place, advance to **Civilization**.

## 4. Civilization — power the settlement and launch the first ship

Choose a doctrine in **Society**. Technological improves ore production and jump range, Arcane boosts charge and insight, Biological supports growth, and Hybrid improves yields across the board.

<details>
<summary>A route using a crystal reactor</summary>

1. In **Lab**, combine **Crystal + Brine** with **Aether flux** at **1** for **Crystal resonance**. If the response is weak, compare local Ψ at another site or raise the intensity. Visit an **Aether spring** when charge runs low.
2. For later spaceflight research, discover **Conductive lattice** with **Ore + Crystal**, **Current**, **1**.
3. In **Build**, construct a **Crystal reactor** for 5 ore and 4 crystal, then a **Shipyard** for 6 ore, 3 crystal and 2 charge.

</details>

A **Biomech nursery** or an **Evolving machine** also satisfies the power goal. All three routes need the shipyard and the crystal resonance discovery to launch. Reserve at least **5 ore, 3 crystal, 2 charge and 1 biomass** for the station and greeting in orbit. Select **Field → Spaceflight →** to launch at your home star.

## 5. Spaceflight — visit a second star

1. In **Atlas → Galaxy**, select **Next star**. Check the distance and your fuel, then press **Jump**. If fuel is low, select **Refuel at star** and wait for the manoeuvre to finish.
2. While still in orbit around the foreign star, open **Society → Greet** for first contact. In **Build**, construct an **Orbital station**.
3. In **Atlas**, select **Land** beside a planet and wait for arrival. On its surface build a **Colony** for 6 biomass, 4 ore and 2 charge. The planet must orbit another star.
4. For the last goal, open **Lab**. The surface of your colony has sites where you can collect more samples.

<details>
<summary>Hint for the spatial fold</summary>

Check the archive for **Crystal resonance** and **Conductive lattice** first. Then select **Ore + Crystal**, **Aether flux**, **3**. The trial also costs 3 charge. If the response is weak at this maximum intensity, try a stronger local field or another planet.

</details>

When all four goals are checked, select **Field → Transcendence →**.

## 6. Transcendence — create a world and return home

**Create a planet.** From the surface choose **Atlas → Launch to orbit**, then **Synthesis → Create planet**. You need 8 ore, 6 charge and 4 crystal. In the planet list the new object is tagged **Constructed world**. Land there and select **Synthesis → Seed ecosystem** for 8 biomass, 4 brine and 3 charge. Open **Archive → Local species** to inspect the new populations.

**Return home.** Launch to orbit and refuel. In **Atlas → Galaxy**, select **Home star**, then press **Jump**. If you have travelled far, take intermediate jumps within range. In the home system, choose **Land** next to the planet marked **Home**. Use the buildings on **Atlas → Planet** and your recorded coordinates to find your settlement. Check your alliance in **Society**.

**Link two worlds.** On the surfaces of two different planets use **Synthesis → Build portal**. Each costs 5 crystal, 6 charge and 2 ore. Select **Approach local portal**, wait until you arrive, and choose the name of the other world. Each transit costs 4 charge.

## If you get stuck

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| The meal counter stays still | Select a bloom once and wait. Manual movement cancels the course. Check the pause indicator and use **P** to resume if needed. |
| The next epoch button is grey | Click an unfinished goal in **Field** to open its panel. |
| The portal or station is missing from the menu | Stations are built in orbit from Spaceflight onwards. Portals are built on the surface in Transcendence. |
| The save slot looks empty after moving the HTML file | Open the old copy in the original browser and choose **Saves → Export file**. In the new location choose **Saves → Import file** and select the `.genesis` file. |
