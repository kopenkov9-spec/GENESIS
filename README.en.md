# GENESIS: Beyond Matter

[Български](README.md) · [Walkthrough](docs/tutorials/PLAY_EN.md) · [Controls](README_EN.md)

You begin as a single cell. Much later, you can fly home, land on the same planet and find the settlement you built on its shore. GENESIS is an offline sandbox about inheritance, discovery and civilizations in a procedural universe. The game is playable in English and Bulgarian.

## Start playing

1. Use **Download raw file** on the [standalone HTML](GENESIS_Beyond_Matter.html), or download and extract the [full project ZIP](https://github.com/kopenkov9-spec/GENESIS/archive/refs/heads/main.zip).
2. Open `GENESIS_Beyond_Matter.html` in your browser. On Windows you can also run [START_GAME.bat](START_GAME.bat) from the extracted folder.
3. To switch from Bulgarian, open **Настройки → Език → English**. Enter a seed of your own or use `PELAGOS`, then choose **Begin a new life**. In **Field**, select **Nutrient bloom** and follow your cell to its first meal.

Once downloaded, the game runs locally. All you need to play is the HTML file and a browser.

## Six epochs in one world

| Epoch | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Genesis | Find food, grow an organ and divide. |
| Evolution | Adapt your descendants to dry land and found a population. |
| Awakening | Build a settlement, make a tool and win an ally. |
| Civilization | Choose a doctrine, power your settlement and prepare a shipyard. |
| Spaceflight | Jump between stars, land, make contact and found colonies. |
| Transcendence | Create worlds and species, connect planets with portals and change local time. |

Each goal under **Field → Next epoch** opens the panel where you can act on it. After the sixth epoch, keep playing in the same save. A seed recreates the starting universe. To carry your own history to another browser or computer, use **Saves → Export file**.
